Sugar-tax spread

Regular price per litre of full-sugar Coca-Cola versus Zero Sugar, matched by pack size, pack count and container, for SKUs listed today. Flavoured and caffeine-free variants are excluded. The levy column compares the premium with the tax on full-sugar drinks (€0.30 per litre including VAT).

A premium above the levy does not show that the tax is over-passed to shoppers, and one below it does not show absorption: the gap also reflects pricing choices for each pack. Read the ratio as a benchmark, not a measured pass-through.

Price per litre by format

Regular shelf price per litre for every SKU listed today. Larger packs lower the cost per litre — the core of price-pack architecture.

Promotions

How often each SKU carried a promotion over the full history, which mechanics were used, and how deep the cut was against the regular price. Multibuy cuts are measured on the per-unit price when the offer is taken.

Price per litre over time

Daily price per litre after promotions, one panel per matched format. Dashed lines show the regular price; where the solid line dips below it, a promotion was running.
