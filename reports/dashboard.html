Methodology

How the data is collected and modelled, and what the numbers can and cannot say.

Collection

Once a day, an automated job reads SuperValu Ireland's public search results for a fixed set of cola, lemon-lime, orange and energy-drink queries, at human rate (one page every 3–7 seconds) and without logging in. Each product card yields the price shown, any promotion badge, the pack size and the brand label. Nothing personal is collected.

A run is refused if it returns fewer than half the products of the past week, so a broken or partial page never enters the history. Re-running a day replaces that day's rows. Obvious retailer typos in pack volume (for example "1.75 ml" for 1.75 L) are corrected and flagged.

Pricing model

  • Price per litre divides the price by the total volume of the pack.
  • Regular price: on days without a promotion, or with a multibuy, the card shows the shelf price. On price-cut, Rewards-price and value-badge days the regular price is carried forward from the latest clean day, and is unknown until one is observed.
  • Price paid today applies the promotional price, or the per-unit price of a multibuy when the offer is taken. Rewards prices require a free SuperValu Real Rewards card and are marked.
  • Like-for-like means same pack size, pack count, container and sugar tier. Cola comparisons exclude flavoured and caffeine-free variants.

Events

Dated events, such as Budget 2027 on 6 October 2026, are marked on the time series and studied automatically: before the date, the latest 14 days of regular prices are held as a baseline; from the date, the 14 days before are compared with the days after. The levy used in every benchmark is the rate in force on the latest run date. A change after an event is reported as a change, not attributed to the event.

What the numbers do and do not say

The levy ratio compares the full-sugar premium with the tax per litre. It is a benchmark, not a measured pass-through: each premium also reflects pricing strategy. Shelf prices show three of the four revenue-growth-management levers — price, pack and mix, promotion. Trade investment, volumes, margins and promotional ROI are not observable here, and no finding claims them.
